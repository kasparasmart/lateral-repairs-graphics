Lateral Repairs Fast Cast FC15

2K unfilled epoxy system for pipe relining · A + B components

Epoxy resinAmbient cure
Mix ratio (weight)

Product description

  • 2K unfilled epoxy system
  • Low shrinkage
  • Fast curing
  • Room temperature curing or moderate hot curing process
  • Slightly thixotropic system specifically designed to reduce migration

Areas of application

Repair and renovation of drain and sewage pipes. Low pressure small pipe relining.

Processing methods

Impregnation on site, with the aid of vacuum, of felt used in the repair and renovation of the pipes. Cured in Place Pipe Process (CIPP) at room temperature curing or at 50 °C for 30 minutes.

Curing / post-curing

Post-curing is not required if the CIPP is performed at the recommended temperatures and times.

Storage and stability

Unfilled epoxy resin and its amine-based hardener can be stored for two years in the original sealed containers stored in a cool, dry place. The hardener is moisture sensitive, therefore it is good practice to close the container immediately after each use.

Handling precautions

Refer to the safety data sheet and comply with regulations relating to industrial health and waste disposal.
